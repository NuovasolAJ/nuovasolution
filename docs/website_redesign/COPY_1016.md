## 1. The morning letter, rewritten

### 1.1 What the delivered letter showed, and what each fault is

Read off the 14.10. screenshot, in the order a reader meets them.

| # | What was on screen | The fault |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Una cosa espera a una persona" | the headline counts instead of saying **what** waits. It would be the same headline for a callback, a viewing or a held reply |
| 2 | "Solicitud de visita: **+4 915 156 766 918**" | the person is a number, and the number is **regrouped wrongly**. `+4915156766918` was cut into threes as if it were Spanish |
| 3 | "Ha escrito: «nächste Woche»" | the customer's German dropped raw into a Spanish sentence, unmarked |
| 4 | "Para que lo sepas: pidió «nächste Woche»" | **the same quote twice**, three lines apart. Also still the old wording this lane replaced on 2026-10-14 (`good_to_know` → "El cliente mencionó") |
| 5 | «nächste Woche» with no origin date | a relative date read days later. "Next week" from which day? |
| 6 | "Lo siguiente: confirmar la visita para la próxima semana." | **suggests an appointment exists.** There is a wish, not a booking. It also silently converts the customer's words into our own claim about a week |
| 7 | headline, "Queda una sola cosa abierta" and the card's state | the same fact said **three times** before anything useful appears |

### 1.2 The headline says what waits, by kind

The headline is the first task's subject, never a count. The count moves to the line under it.

| case | ES | EN |
|---|---|---|
| viewing wish, with a name | {customer} quiere ver un inmueble | {customer} wants to see a property |
| viewing wish, no name | Visita pendiente de acordar | A viewing still to be agreed |
| callback, with a name | {customer} espera tu llamada | {customer} is waiting for your call |
| callback, no name | Llamada pendiente de devolver | A call still to be returned |
| held reply, with a name | {customer} sigue sin respuesta | {customer} has not been answered |
| held reply, no name | Un cliente sigue sin respuesta | A customer has not been answered |
| nothing open | Esta mañana no hay nada esperando | Nothing is waiting this morning |

**Where no name exists, the subject is the request, not the person.** "Una cosa" and "alguien" are both
ways of saying we did not look.

### 1.3 The rest of the slots

| Slot | ES | EN |
|---|---|---|
| EYEBROW | Tu mañana en {agencia} | Your morning at {agency} |
| SUBJECT | *the headline of 1.2, unchanged* | *the same* |
| GREETING | Buenos días, {nombre}. | Good morning, {name}. |
| SUMMARY, one task | Es lo único abierto esta mañana. | It is the only thing open this morning. |
| SUMMARY, several | Y {n−1} más esperan detrás. | And {n−1} more are waiting behind it. |
| NEXT_ACTION, viewing wish | Lo siguiente: acordar una hora con {customer}. | Next: agree a time with {customer}. |
| NEXT_ACTION, callback | Lo siguiente: devolver la llamada. | Next: return the call. |
| NEXT_ACTION, held reply | Lo siguiente: contestar tú al cliente; el borrador está en la tarea. | Next: answer the customer yourself; the draft is in the task. |
| BUTTON | Abrir mis tareas | Open my tasks |
| FOOTER | Lo recibes porque trabajas en {agencia}. Las tareas se toman y se cierran en tu lista. | You receive this because you work at {agency}. Tasks are taken and closed in your list. |

**"Confirmar la visita" is struck from every slot.** A wish is agreed, never confirmed: the word
"confirmar" belongs to a booking that exists, and none does. This is the same rule the staff card already
follows and the website has followed since 2026-09-29.

### 1.4 The card: the customer's words and our summary, in two blocks

The single biggest change. Today they are one sentence; they become two blocks that can never be confused.

| Block | ES | EN |
|---|---|---|
| Row title, with a name | {kind} · {customer} | {kind} · {customer} |
| Row title, no name | {kind} · {número tal cual} | {kind} · {number as stored} |
| Our summary | Pide una visita. Escribió en alemán. | Asks for a viewing. Wrote in German. |
| The customer's own words | **Lo que escribió, en sus palabras:** «nächste Woche» · *mensaje del 12 oct* | **In the customer's own words:** «nächste Woche» · *message of 12 Oct* |
| Why now, from the envelope | Por qué ahora: {summary.headline} | Why now: {summary.headline} |
| State | Pendiente · nadie la ha tomado | Open · nobody has taken it |
| Link | Abrir | Open |

**Four rules the blocks carry:**

1. **A quotation is never translated and never paraphrased inside our sentence.** It sits in its own
   block, in quotation marks, in the language it was written in, with the date it was written. Our summary
   says *that* the customer wrote in German, not *what* German means.
2. **A relative expression keeps its origin date.** «nächste Woche» becomes readable only as
   "«nächste Woche» · mensaje del 12 oct". We never resolve it into a calendar week of our own, because
   the customer's week counts from their message, not from the morning somebody reads the letter.
3. **A phone number is printed exactly as it is stored**, in E.164, `+4915156766918`. Never regrouped. A
   wrong grouping of a foreign number is worse than no grouping: it looks like a different number.
4. **`good_to_know` renders only if its content is not already in the quotation block.** The duplicate in
   the delivered letter came from printing the same fragment twice through two different keys.

## 2. The weekly report

A short balance first, then what was measured, then what is open with its next step. The deepening lives
behind the login **and is only linked once that path has actually been accepted** — today it is not, so
the button points at the list the reader already uses.

| Slot | ES | EN |
|---|---|---|
| SUBJECT, with answers | Tu semana en {agencia}: {answered} consultas atendidas | Your week at {agency}: {answered} enquiries answered |
| SUBJECT, no answers yet | Tu semana en {agencia} | Your week at {agency} |
| OPENING | Del {desde} al {hasta}, por {canales}. | From {from} to {to}, through {channels}. |
| THE BALANCE, one line | Llegaron {received} consultas y se atendieron {answered}. Quedan {open_actions} esperando a una persona. | {received} enquiries arrived and {answered} were answered. {open_actions} are waiting for a person. |
| RESULTS | Se pidieron {viewings} visitas y {valuations} valoraciones. Tu equipo cerró {tasks} tareas. | {viewings} viewings and {valuations} valuations were requested. Your team closed {tasks} tasks. |
| THE OPEN ONES, heading | Lo que sigue abierto | What is still open |
| each open item | {kind} · {customer} — {next step} | {kind} · {customer} — {next step} |
| COMPARISON, 5 or more | La semana pasada fueron {prev}. | Last week it was {prev}. |
| COMPARISON, below 5 | Semana pasada: {prev}. Son pocas para sacar una tendencia. | Last week: {prev}. Too few to read a trend into. |
| BUTTON | Abrir mis tareas | Open my tasks |
| CLOSE | Si algo no cuadra, responde a este correo. | If something does not add up, reply to this mail. |

**The zero week** keeps its own three lines from `WEEKLY_REPORT_MAIL_v1.md` §4; they are unchanged and
still right.

**Missing measurements, handled without turning the mail into an error log.** The rule tightens: a field
that is absent produces **no line at all**, and there is **at most one** closing sentence about what is
not measured, used **only** where its absence would otherwise read as a zero:

| ES | EN |
|---|---|
| La entrega al cliente todavía no la medimos, así que esta semana no la contamos. | We do not measure delivery to the customer yet, so we are not counting it this week. |

Everything else is simply absent. A reader does not need an inventory of our instrumentation.

**Never in this mail:** revenue, saved time, a delivery we did not measure, a conversion rate, an
advertising recommendation, or a figure the contract does not carry. `provider_accepted` and `delivered`
stay apart, and neither renders today.

## 6. The dictation privacy line → Daily, then counsel

From the measured flow: recognition is the browser's Web Speech API; the audio goes to **the browser
maker's** recognition service (Chrome to Google, Safari to Apple); the level meter is local and stores
nothing; nothing is sent without *Enviar*.

| Where | ES | EN |
|---|---|---|
| under the microphone, one line | El dictado lo hace tu navegador: el audio va al servicio de reconocimiento de su fabricante (Chrome, Google; Safari, Apple), no a nosotros. Nosotros recibimos el texto solo cuando pulsas Enviar. | Dictation is done by your browser: the audio goes to its maker's recognition service (Chrome, Google; Safari, Apple), not to us. We receive the text only when you press Send. |

**Why it is worded that way:** we are not the recipient of the audio and we have no contract for it, so a
line that says "we process your voice" would be false in the other direction. What the person needs to
know is that speaking into this field sends audio to a third party they already have a relationship with,
and that we see only the text. **To counsel, with the staff client:** whether this placement and wording
suffice, since the speaker is an employee rather than a customer.
