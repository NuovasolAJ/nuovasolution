# PRODUCT TEXTS C3 v1 — redesign copy, EN and ES

**State:** `2026-09-22_SYNC_1800Z` · **Written:** 2026-09-23 · **Copy version:** `redesign-v1`
**Lane:** Website Copy / Product Truth → **Website Implementer**; Reviewer checks it against the
rendered candidate.
**Status:** DRAFT by its author, not independently reviewed.
**Why now:** `OWNER_VISUAL_ACCEPTANCE = REJECTED`; the bright redesign is mandatory, and the
current copy is too long and too internal for it.

> Every section is one customer question, one heading of at most eight words, one or two short
> sentences, and an optional detail the visitor can open. Word counts are guides, not rules. The
> Spanish is written for Spain, not translated word for word. Qualifiers sit with the claim they
> qualify, never in a footer (this is what review findings WR-33 and WR-34 are about).

**Evidence rule for this file.** No sentence is stronger than
`CLOSEOUT_EVIDENCE_INDEX_v1.md`. What is proven in production today: text enquiries on WhatsApp
and Gmail answered automatically, the enquiry recorded as a lead with a qualification and a
priority, a viewing request becoming a task. Everything else is named with its real status.

### 3.3 How paying works, stated plainly

The contracted path today is a request and an invoice paid by bank transfer. There is **no
automatic card subscription**, and a sent invoice does not unlock anything: only a confirmed
payment does (`API_SYNC_0922_RETURN_v1.md`; `PROMPT_02_API.md` A4).

| Step | EN | ES |

| 2 | We send you an invoice. | Te enviamos una factura. |
| 3 | You pay it by bank transfer. | La pagas por transferencia. |
| 4 | When the payment is confirmed, the plan is active. | Cuando el pago está confirmado, el plan queda activo. |

Stated once, near the steps, not in a footnote:

| EN | ES |
|---|---|
| There is no card checkout and no automatic renewal. Receiving an invoice does not activate the plan; the confirmed payment does. | No hay pago con tarjeta ni renovación automática. Recibir la factura no activa el plan; lo activa el pago confirmado. |

## 7. What this file deliberately does not say

- No response time, no number of enquiries, no conversion or revenue claim.
- No hot lead alert, in any wording or visual (`CLAIMS_MATRIX.md` D-10).
- No automatic follow up. It is not sent in production and E-01 is legally held.
- No portal or web form intake, and no named CRM vendor (owner decision D2 still open).
- No price, interval or discount.
- No claim that an enquiry always gets a reply. "Text enquiries on connected channels" is the
  honest scope.
