Neither is copied as code. The stage follows the owner's rules instead: native scrolling, both directions, information still when the
scroll stops, navigation / jump links / keyboard / the phone bar independent of the film, the first screen from a small poster, media
per section, stills for reduced motion, slow devices or connections and load errors, all eight sections and every action complete
without the film.

## 3. Redesign's plan for the prototype (local, stills)

1. **One stage behind the page.** A fixed, full-viewport layer (`position: fixed; inset: 0; z-index: 0`) carries the house; the eight
   sections scroll over it as today, so no extra scroll distance exists and nothing is pinned. The stage shows the scene that belongs to
   the section under the reader and crossfades to the next one over the section edge (transform + opacity only; one small JS driver,
   passive scroll listener + rAF, writes styles directly; CSS scroll-driven animations are the later upgrade where supported).
2. **Scene map (7 scenes → 8 sections)** exactly as the owner's table: B1 living room · B2 stairs (pain at the lower landing, solution at
   the sunlit landing) · B3+B4 sitting room (the camera stands while the four cards run) · B5 study · B6 gallery · B7 balcony hold · B8
   balcony → pool (the flight begins at the transition to the close).
3. **Readable product layers.** Every section keeps its HTML; text sits on the house's "calm readable area" or on a light limestone /
   glass panel where the picture is busy. Contrast is measured on the lightest and the darkest picture used, in both languages.
4. **Phone.** Own crops per scene (object-position per breakpoint), the fixed bar never over an action (probe as for E2).
5. **Fallbacks.** Poster at once (the first scene, small); scenes 2–7 load when their section nears; reduced motion / Save-Data / slow
   device / load error → the stills stand, no crossfade; everything usable.
6. **Stills for the prototype:** free licensed photos (Pexels License) of similar rooms, one per scene, recorded in
   `FOTOS_LIZENZ_1124_v1.md` as prototype placeholders — not one house yet. The one fictional villa comes from the 3D lane's render
   (own start frame, no licence question) or from a generator the owner pays for after the collected offer.
7. **Later:** the film (video scrubbed by `currentTime`, or a frame sequence drawn per section) replaces the stills behind the same
   sections; the logo on the pool floor only in post-production.
