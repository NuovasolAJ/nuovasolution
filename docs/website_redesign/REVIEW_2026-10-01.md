## 2. Findings on the delivered design preview

**F1 · Small meta text below the contrast floor (P2).**
Route: `/en`, `/es`, `/en/platform`, `/es/platform`, `/es/packages` — 20 route/viewport combinations at 360, 390, 768, 1024 px.
Visible effect: the 13 px muted labels of the product surfaces are hard to read: `rgb(119,115,108)` on `rgb(243,241,236)` = **4.18 : 1**, on the green task card `rgb(234,241,230)` = **4.09 : 1**; WCAG AA asks 4.5 : 1 for this size. Examples: "WhatsApp · new enquiry", "Laura Serrano · Sunday 21:40", "WhatsApp · consulta nueva".
Screenshot: `rvd-390-en-home-scrolled.png`, `rvd-768-en-home-preview-badge.png`.
Closing condition: darken the muted token (or enlarge these labels) until every text under 18.66 px reaches 4.5 : 1 — re-measured on the deployment, both languages.

**F2 · Spanish labels clipped in buttons and chips (P2).**
Route: `/es`, `/es/platform`, `/es/packages` at 768 and 1024 px.
Visible effect: the text box is wider than its container, so the label is cut: "Prueba Essential gratis" 166 px in a 156 px box, "Solicita una propuesta" 163 / 156, the chip "WhatsApp" 64 / 60 at 1024 px.
Closing condition: the Spanish labels fit without clipping at 768 and 1024 px (the Spanish string is the longer one; EN alone is not a test).

**F3 · The product assistant answers nothing (P1, product).**
Route: staging alias, `/en` and `/es`, assistant panel.
Reproduction: ask "Does Nuova answer WhatsApp enquiries at night?" (EN), "¿Tengo que cambiar de CRM?" (ES), "What does Essential cost?" (EN).
Visible effect, 3.6 s each: **"We cannot confirm that from here. A person can." / "No podemos confirmarlo desde aquí. Una persona sí puede."** plus a "Contact a person" link. The AI notice is present and correct ("Answers are written by an AI assistant."). The endpoint answers `cannot_confirm` immediately, with no pending state.
This is the question the order says to lead as **FAIL**, and the two other questions fail the same way. All three are covered by the product FAQ, so the fallback is not a knowledge gap in the question — the assistant path itself does not deliver.
Screenshots: `rvd-qa2-en-night.png`, `rvd-qa2-es-crm.png`, `rvd-qa2-en-price.png`.
Closing condition: `WEBSITE_QA_STAGING = PASS` with a **visible product answer** to these three questions in both languages, plus the negative cases (unknown question → human **only** with a recorded contact, timeout, foreign tenant, prompt injection) and no property-lead side effect.
