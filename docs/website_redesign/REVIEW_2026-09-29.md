### Content baseline of both previews (identical text, 12 routes)

- **No** "checkout", **no** "coming soon", **no** absolute claims ("never miss", "always synced", "100%"), **no**
  "setup in five minutes" — those external findings are not reproducible on this state.
- Packages state the payment path honestly: "An issued invoice does not activate anything. The confirmed payment
  does." Bank transfer, no online payment.
- **No** "14 days free" sticker inside the Growth or Scale cards; the trial is a page-level statement. What is still
  missing is the binding to Essential (`TRIAL_PLAN_ALIGNED`), so the trial is not yet tied to a plan in the wording.
- **Plan limits print "CRM connections 1 / 3 / 10"** (Essential / Growth / Scale). Against `PRODUCT_TRUTH_TABLE`
  (0 active external CRM flows) this advertises an integration depth that is not evidenced — carried into R2 as a
  claim finding (external finding 8).
- "Legal pages are published as labelled placeholders pending counsel." is an honest label, not a defect; the legal
  pages themselves carry `PLACEHOLDER` markers (finding 17, open until `LEGAL_PAGES_FINAL v2`).
- Spanish legal routes are served under the English slugs (`/es/legal/privacy`); `/es/legal/privacidad` is 404.
  Minor, but a Spanish visitor sees an English URL.
