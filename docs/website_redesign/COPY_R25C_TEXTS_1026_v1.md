## 1. Voice: phone texts in English and German

Built on the only approved phone text, `v2.0-es-voice-2026-10`: "Hola, le atiende el asistente de inteligencia artificial de
NuovaSolution, un sistema automático. ¿En qué puedo ayudarle?" The same rules hold in every language: the AI is named **and**
called an automated system; no promise of a transfer (there is none); no variable inside a text (the agency name is
written out per agency); no time promised; a wished time is a wish.

**Register:** German uses **Sie** on the phone. English has no choice to make.

### V-1 · Opening, with the AI notice

E10 chooses the form for Spanish: (a) with a first name after the notice, (b) without one, or the current form naming
NuovaSolution. **English and German follow whatever E10 decides for Spanish**; both forms are given.

| | EN | DE |
|---|---|---|
| names NuovaSolution (mirror of v2.0) | Hello, you are speaking with NuovaSolution's artificial intelligence assistant, an automated system. How can I help you? | Guten Tag, Sie sprechen mit dem KI-Assistenten von NuovaSolution, einem automatischen System. Wie kann ich Ihnen helfen? |
| names the agency, E10 (b) | Hello, you have reached Marbella Costa Propiedades. You are speaking with the agency's artificial intelligence assistant, an automated system. How can I help you? | Guten Tag, Sie haben Marbella Costa Propiedades angerufen. Sie sprechen mit dem KI-Assistenten der Agentur, einem automatischen System. Wie kann ich Ihnen helfen? |

"Marbella Costa Propiedades" stands for the agency's name, written out in each agency's row.

### V-2 · Switching language when the caller asks

The notice is repeated in the new language, because the caller may not have understood it the first time.

| into | text |
|---|---|
| ES | Claro, seguimos en español. Para que lo sepa: soy el asistente de inteligencia artificial de la agencia, un sistema automático. |
| EN | Of course, let's continue in English. Just so you know, I am the agency's artificial intelligence assistant, an automated system. |
| DE | Gern, sprechen wir auf Deutsch weiter. Damit Sie es wissen: Ich bin der KI-Assistent der Agentur, ein automatisches System. |

If E10 keeps the form naming NuovaSolution, "de la agencia" / "the agency's" / "der Agentur" becomes NuovaSolution's.

### V-3 · When the language is unclear

First the sentence in the language of the greeting, then the English one, once.

| EN | Sorry, I did not quite understand you. I can help you in Spanish, English or German. |
| DE | Entschuldigung, ich habe Sie nicht gut verstanden. Ich kann Ihnen auf Spanisch, Englisch oder Deutsch helfen. |

### V-4 · When the caller asks for a language the phone does not offer

| | text |
|---|---|
| ES | Lo siento, por teléfono puedo atenderle en español, en inglés o en alemán. Si lo prefiere, dejo anotado que una persona de la agencia le devuelva la llamada. |
| EN | I'm sorry, on the phone I can help you in Spanish, English or German. If you prefer, I will note that a person from the agency should call you back. |
| DE | Es tut mir leid, am Telefon kann ich Ihnen auf Spanisch, Englisch oder Deutsch helfen. Wenn Sie möchten, notiere ich, dass eine Person aus der Agentur Sie zurückruft. |

V-3 and V-4 replace today's fixed sentence "Por ahora le atiendo solo en español. ¿Seguimos en español?" **only when EN and DE
are switched on and accepted**. Until then the current sentence stays, because it is the true one.

### V-5 · A wished time, and the close

A wish is noted, a person confirms (KALENDER). Nothing says when the call will come.

| | EN | DE | ES, for the same structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| wished time | I have noted that you would prefer {time}. A person from the agency will call you to confirm it. | Ich habe notiert, dass Ihnen {time} am liebsten wäre. Eine Person aus der Agentur ruft Sie an, um das zu bestätigen. | He anotado que prefiere {time}. Una persona de la agencia le llamará para confirmarlo. |
| callback without a time | I have noted it, and a person from the agency will call you back. | Ich habe es notiert, und eine Person aus der Agentur ruft Sie zurück. | Lo he anotado, y una persona de la agencia le devolverá la llamada. |
| close | Thank you for calling. Goodbye. | Vielen Dank für Ihren Anruf. Auf Wiederhören. | Gracias por su llamada. Hasta luego. |

`{time}` is said back as the caller said it, with the time zone in the record, not in speech. The Spanish column replaces
"Un colega le llamará en breve": "en breve" promises a timing no one has measured.

true by construction.
